# Launch page (merged waitlist)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: anyone who works at a computer all day. Action: leave an email. Constraint: one product, shown as one app; no "example" labels; no screenshots as images.

## Direction contract

THESIS: You land and see your laptop's day turn into a calendar. Refuses the feature-grid landing page with three separate app windows.

OWN-WORLD: Superlogical's paper (#f5f5f4) and ink, Geist, one warm accent; Ramp's mouse-driven sand field behind the hero; the Daylens app itself (Ship's log tokens, navy ink) as the only container.

STORY: The visitor sees the raw stream of apps, windows and pages from one Wednesday, watches it collapse into a clean calendar beside what their calendar said, sees search and assistants act on the same calendar, then reads why in a short essay and joins.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Name top left, join link top right. Centered headline saying what Daylens does, one line under it, the email field ("What's your email?") with its button. Sand field behind. The app window's top edge rises from the bottom of the viewport.

FORM: Pinned scroll stage (Toggl's enlarge) on a single app window; user-pinned direction, no seed roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
