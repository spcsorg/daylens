# Design System: Daylens V2

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Logbook on Paper"**

The page is a sheet of warm paper with dark ink on it, and the only object on that sheet is the Daylens app. Everything Daylens claims is shown inside one app window that behaves like the real product: a raw log of what the computer reported, turning into a calendar of what you did, next to what your calendar said, then searched and read by assistants. Outside the window the page is quiet reading: one headline, one line, an email field, a short essay, two cards, a closing line.

Two material worlds meet and never blend. The page uses greenish-neutral paper and ink with one warm orange accent. The window uses the Daylens app's own Ship's log tokens from the Daylens-V2 Figma file (navy ink, soft cream surfaces, pale blue blocks). A page never borrows app tokens for its own chrome, and the window never borrows page tokens, except the accent, which marks a search hit inside the app.

Density is low on paper and true-to-product inside the window. Behind the hero, a field of sand grains settles into slow patterns and reorganises as the mouse moves between screen quadrants; it is texture, not content, and it is masked clear of the headline.

This file describes the launch page (`app/(launch)/`), including its sand backdrop (`_launch/Backdrop.tsx`, `field.ts`).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Paper and ink outside, Ship's log inside one app window.
- One warm accent, used for focus, selection, the typing caret, the never-records strike, and search hits. Never for buttons or text.
- Geist for every word; Geist Mono only for raw lines the computer reported verbatim.
- Product shown as live HTML, never as screenshot images.
- One pinned scroll stage per page at most; everything else is static reading.
- Light and dark follow the operating system.

## Colors

A greenish-neutral paper and ink page with one warm orange, wrapped around a navy-on-cream app with pale blue blocks; one saturated blue card carries the "what it records" list.

### Primary
- **Ember Orange** (#e8643c; dark #ff8a62): the accent. Focus outlines (2px, 3px offset), the typing caret in search, the strike line through "never records" icons, and the border of calendar blocks that match a search. Its soft form, **Ember Wash** (#fd937133; dark #ff8a6233), is text selection and the 3px ring around a matched block.

### Secondary
- **Beam Blue** (#3559c4; dark #2a4896): the background of the "what it records" card, with **Beam Light** (rgb(160 190 245); dark rgb(60 98 186)) pooling at 55% into its bottom-right corner as a radial light. White text on it (6.2:1 light, 8.5:1 dark). It appears on one card per page.

### Neutral (page)
- **Warm Paper** (#f5f5f4; dark #10120f): page background, nav fill once scrolled, button text, theme-color meta.
- **Field Ink** (#181d16; dark #ececea): headings, body, primary buttons.
- **Field Ink Soft** (#4b5147; dark #b9bdb4): the line under the headline, the second essay, the "never" list, closing sub-line.
- **Moss Grey** (#686d64; dark #8a9086): placeholder text, footer, struck "never" icons. 4.9:1 on paper.
- **Pencil Rule** (#181d161f; dark #ececea24): hairlines (nav underline once scrolled, field border, "never" card border, footer rule).
- **Card White** (#ffffff; dark #181b20): the "never records" card surface.
- **Kiln Red** (#c2412d; dark #ff8f7a): form error text and error border only.

### Neutral (in-window, Ship's log)
- **Log Cream** (#fafaf7; dark #17191e): window surface, search, answer, and assistants panels.
- **Title Bar Linen** (#f1f0eb; dark #1e2127): window title bar.
- **Log Navy** (#14213d; dark #e8ebf2): primary in-app text and the tint of every in-window shadow.
- **Navy Soft** (#4e5871; dark #aab1c2): raw line text, calendar event text.
- **Navy Faint** (#6b7287; dark #8c93a5): timestamps, hour labels, column labels, date, icon strokes. 4.6:1 on Log Cream.
- **Ledger Line** (#e4e2db; dark #2b2f37): window border, title bar divider, hour rules, panel borders.
- **Block Blue** (#e9eefb; dark #22304f) with **Block Edge** (#c9d5f4; dark #34477a): a calendar block of what you did.
- **Event Sand** (#f3f1ea; dark #23252b) with **Event Edge** (#dcd8cc; dark #383b43): a calendar event of what was planned.
- **Traffic lights** (#ff5f57, #febc2e, #28c840): the three 12px window dots, in both themes.

### Named Rules
**The Two Inks Rule.** Page text is Field Ink; window text is Log Navy. A surface is either paper or app, never a mix.

**The Ember Is a Signal Rule.** The accent marks focus, selection, typing, exclusion, and a search hit. It is never a fill for buttons, links, headings, or backgrounds.

**The One Blue Card Rule.** Beam Blue fills one card per page, the one that states what Daylens does. Its partner card is Card White with a Pencil Rule border.

### Dark mode

Dark mode follows the operating system's preference; there is no toggle. Every token above has a dark pair in the frontmatter (the `-dark` keys), swapped wholesale under `prefers-color-scheme: dark`. The page becomes near-black paper (#10120f) with light ink; the window becomes a blue-black app with deep blue blocks; the accent warms to #ff8a62; Beam Blue deepens. The email field takes its own dark surface (#1a1d18), the sand dims to 35%, and theme-color matches the paper in each scheme.

**Body Font:** Geist

**Character:** One grotesque in three weights, tight tracking at size and plain at reading size. Headings are medium weight (500), never bold; 600 is reserved for the wordmark.

- **Raw** (Geist Mono 400, 13px; 10.5px on phones): raw activity lines only.

### Named Rules
**The Verbatim Mono Rule.** Geist Mono is only for text the computer reported verbatim: a timestamp and a window or page title in the raw log. Never for labels, numbers in prose, code-flavoured decoration, or headings.

**The Medium Headline Rule.** Headings are Geist 500 with negative tracking. Nothing on the page uses 700.

## Layout

The page is a single centred column on paper. A fixed 64px nav sits on top with the wordmark left and a "Join the waitlist" pill right, both inset by clamp(16px, 4vw, 40px). The nav is transparent over the stage and becomes Warm Paper with a Pencil Rule underline once the stage has scrolled past.

**The stage.** The first section is one pinned scroll stage: a 380svh-tall track with a 100svh sticky viewport. The hero (headline, lead, email field) sits centred at the top of that viewport, padded by 64px plus clamp(32px, 8svh, 96px). The app window's top edge starts at 64svh (66svh on phones), so it rises from the bottom of the first viewport. As the visitor scrolls, the window grows to fill the screen: top 72px (64px on phones), width from min(1040px, 82vw) to min(1320px, 96vw) (phones: 100vw minus 32px to 100vw minus 16px), height 100svh minus 88px (minus 72px on phones).

**Inside the window.** Padding is clamp(16px, 3vw, 36px). The calendar has a time axis column (64px; 40px on phones) for hour labels, Daylens blocks across 66% of the width (60% on phones), and calendar events in the remaining column starting 8px (6px) after. Hours are positioned proportionally to minutes in the day, with a Ledger Line rule per hour.

**Below the stage.** Reading on paper with gutters of clamp(16px, 5vw, 48px) and a top pad of clamp(80px, 12vw, 160px). Essays max 40rem. Cards sit in a 1.15fr / 1fr grid with a 20px gap, max 64rem, single column on phones. Section spacing steps up as the page closes: clamp(64px, 9vw, 112px), clamp(72px, 10vw, 128px), clamp(96px, 14vw, 176px). The footer is a 64rem row with a Pencil Rule top border, copyright left and the join link right.

**The sand.** The backdrop is fixed behind everything at z-index 0, with a faint 10% warm vignette at the edges in light mode. A radial mask keeps the headline and lead clear of grains; its centre is tied to the hero's padding, so a page that moves its headline must move the mask with it. On phones the clear zone spans the full width.

**Breakpoints:** phones up to 767px; 768px and up for the one-line short block; 1024px and up for one-line captions.

### Named Rules
**The One Container Rule.** The app window is the only framed container in the stage. No feature grids, no three windows side by side, no device mockups.

**The Rising Window Rule.** On the first view, the window's top edge is visible at the bottom of the viewport. The story starts on scroll, not on load.

## Elevation & Depth

The paper is flat. Depth belongs to the app: the window floats on a long, soft, navy-tinted shadow, and the panels that appear over the calendar (search, answer, assistants) lift above it with tighter shadows of the same tint. On paper, only the email field lifts, and only slightly. Cards are flat: one is solid blue, the other a white sheet with a hairline.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Window float** (`box-shadow: 0 30px 80px -30px rgb(20 33 61 / 0.35), 0 2px 6px rgb(20 33 61 / 0.06)`): the app window.
- **Field lift** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 24px -12px rgb(24 29 22 / 0.25)`): the email field on paper.
- **Search float** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -14px rgb(20 33 61 / 0.4)`): the in-app search field.
- **Answer float** (`box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -20px rgb(20 33 61 / 0.45)`): the answer panel under search.
- **Assistants float** (`box-shadow: 0 20px 50px -24px rgb(20 33 61 / 0.45)`): the assistants panel.
- **Match ring** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px` Ember Wash): a calendar block that answers the search; a ring, not a shadow.

### Named Rules
**The Navy Shadow Rule.** Every shadow inside the window is tinted Log Navy (rgb 20 33 61), never neutral black. Shadows only hang under things that float above the calendar.

**The Flat Paper Rule.** Nothing on paper has a shadow except the email field. No glass, no blur, no backdrop-filter anywhere.

## Shapes

Soft rectangles that get rounder as they get bigger and further from the data. Icons and app logos inside the window are 4px; assistant icons 6px; the wordmark icon 7px; assistant rows 9px; blocks, events, search, and the field's button 10px; the answer panel 12px; the email field and assistants panel 14px; the app window 20px, tightening to 14px as it fills the screen; the paper cards 28px. Buttons on paper are full pills (999px) except the one inside the email field, which follows the field's inner curve (10px inside 14px with 5px padding). Borders are 1px hairlines. Line icons are drawn on a 20px grid with a 1.5px round stroke and inherit text colour.

## Components

### Buttons
Quiet, solid ink.
- **Shape:** full pill (999px) in the nav; 10px inside the email field.
- **Primary:** Field Ink background, Warm Paper text, Geist 500 15px. Nav pill padding 8px 14px; field button 0 18px at 44px tall.
- **Hover / Focus:** opacity to 0.85 to 0.88 over 0.2s; focus is the 2px Ember Orange outline at 3px offset. Disabled while sending: opacity 0.55 and a progress cursor, with the label switching from "Join the waitlist" to "Joining".
- There are no secondary or ghost buttons. Text links in the footer are plain, Moss Grey, turning Field Ink on hover.

### Inputs / Fields
The email field is one capsule holding the input and its button.
- **Style:** max 440px wide, 5px padding, 6px gap, Pencil Rule border, 14px radius, background Warm Paper mixed 88% with white (dark: #1a1d18), Field lift shadow. The input is borderless, 16px, padded 12px, placeholder "What's your email?" in Moss Grey, with a visually hidden label of the same text.
- **Focus:** the border turns Field Ink over 0.2s.
- **Error:** the border turns Kiln Red and a 14px Kiln Red message appears 10px below, announced as an alert and linked with aria-describedby.
- **Done:** the whole form is replaced by a 17px status line in Field Ink: "You're on the list. Check your inbox for a welcome email."
- The same field appears twice: in the hero and at the close.

### Navigation
- Fixed, 64px. Left: the Daylens icon (28px, 7px radius) and "Daylens" in Geist 600 19px, -0.02em, linking to the top. Right: the primary pill linking to the closing signup.
- Transparent over the stage; Warm Paper with a Pencil Rule underline once scrolled past, transitioning over 0.25s. Same layout on phones.

### Cards
- **Corner Style:** 28px.
- **Records card:** Beam Blue with Beam Light pooling into the bottom-right corner, white headline and list, white line icons.
- **Never card:** Card White, Pencil Rule border, Field Ink headline, Field Ink Soft list. Each icon is drawn in Moss Grey with a diagonal Ember Orange strike.
- **Internal Padding:** clamp(24px, 3vw, 40px). Lists start 28px under the title, 14px between items, 22px icons with a 12px gap.

### App window (signature)
The Daylens app, built in HTML, as the page's only container.
- **Frame:** Log Cream surface, 1px Ledger Line border, 20px radius (14px when full), Window float shadow, clipped.
- **Title bar:** 38px, Title Bar Linen, Ledger Line divider, three 12px traffic-light dots 8px apart at 16px from the left. No title text.
- **Head:** the step caption in Caption type (Log Navy), then the date in Small type, Navy Faint, 6px below ("Wednesday, 3 June").
- **Body:** 44px top padding to leave room for column labels and the search field.
- **Hour axis:** a Ledger Line rule per hour across the full width, with the hour label (Hour type, Navy Faint) sitting on the line, knocked out with Log Cream behind it.
- **Column labels:** "Daylens" over the blocks and "Your calendar" over the events, Label type, Navy Faint. These are in-app column headers, not section labels.

A plain-language title (App block type, Log Navy, two lines max) above a row of 16px app icons. Blocks under an hour put title and icons on one line (from 768px up); blocks under half an hour do so at every width. On phones, short blocks drop their icons.

### Sand backdrop (signature)
- A canvas of grains (about 0.042 per pixel) that settle along the zero lines of one of four fields: a twelve-lobed clock, a lattice, timeline-like horizontal strata, and rings. The lattice shows by default.
- Core grains are 2x2 pixels with a lit and a shaded corner in warm greys; about a fifth are single-pixel dust. A 0.35px blur softens them.
- With a fine pointer that can hover, moving the mouse into another screen quadrant regathers the grains into that quadrant's field. Moving over buttons, links, and inputs does not.
- Fades in over 0.6s once built after load during idle time. Opacity 1 in light mode, 0.35 in dark mode, where the vignette is dropped.

### Pinned stage motion

The stage is driven by scroll, not by time. The only time-based motion on the page is the caret blink, the caption and button transitions, and the sand settling.

- **Scroll follow:** the stage's progress eases toward the scroll position with exponential smoothing (each frame closes 1 − e^(−9·dt) of the gap, dt capped at 0.1s), so a wheel flick reads as motion, not a jump.
- **Phases,** as fractions of the pinned distance, with rests between them that hold the finished state: grow 0 to 0.08, raw log to calendar 0.10 to 0.28, calendar events 0.36 to 0.44, search 0.52 to 0.62, assistants 0.72 to 0.80.
- **Captions switch after their phase is mostly drawn,** at about 60% of it: 0.21, 0.41, 0.58, 0.77. The outgoing caption fades over 0.35s and the incoming one rises 8px over 0.45s on the standard ease.
- **Grow:** the hero fades out 1.6 times as fast as the window grows and lifts 48px; it becomes hidden and inert past 35% of the grow.
- **Raw log to calendar:** each raw line, staggered by 0.035, travels from its log row into its block's position and size, its text fading out within the first third of the move while its background fills with Block Blue and its corners round to 10px. Lines that belong to no block (the locked screen) fade out by mid-phase. Blocks then scale from 0.97 to 1 and fade in.
- **Calendar:** events rise 14px and fade in; the "Your calendar" label fades in with them.
- **Search:** the field drops in 6px, the question types out by mid-phase, the answer rises 8px late in the phase, and matching blocks light up once search passes 55%.
- **Assistants:** the panel rises 24px; its rows slide in 12px from the right, staggered by 0.18. Search and answer fade away as the panel arrives, and the dimmed blocks recover.
- **Standard ease:** cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) for every CSS transition (0.2s for button opacity and field border, 0.25s for the nav, 0.35s to 0.45s for captions, 0.3s for the match ring, 0.6s for the sand fade-in). Sand grains travel 1.09s on a cubic ease-out with up to 0.35s of random delay.
- **Reduced motion:** the stage stops pinning and becomes a static section: the hero, then the finished window at full width (760px tall; 640px on phones) showing the calendar, the events, and the assistants panel, with the caption "Daylens turns it into a calendar of what you actually did." The sand still renders but does not follow the mouse.

#### Named Rules
**The One Stage Rule.** A page gets at most one pinned scroll stage, and only the app window performs in it. Everything after the stage is still.

**The Finished State Rule.** Every frame between phases is a complete, readable screen of the app. Reduced motion shows the finished day, not a blank or a first frame.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** show the product as live HTML built from the in-window tokens, inside one app window with traffic lights and a 38px title bar.
- **Do** keep page tokens (paper, ink, rule, accent) outside the window and Ship's log tokens inside it.
- **Do** set every heading in Geist 500 with negative tracking; reserve 600 for the wordmark.
- **Do** use Geist Mono only for raw timestamps and window or page titles the computer reported.
- **Do** keep the accent to focus, selection, the caret, the never-strike, and search hits.
- **Do** give every scroll phase a rest after it, and switch its caption at about 60% of the phase.
- **Do** ship a reduced-motion version that shows the finished state, and dark pairs for every new token.
- **Do** keep inputs at 16px and buttons at 44px tall on phones.
- **Do** present the illustrative day (a bakery owner's Wednesday, 3 June) as the app's content, unlabelled.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use gradient text, glass, blur panels, or backdrop-filter.
- **Don't** put a small label or kicker above a heading. Headings stand alone.
- **Don't** use em dashes anywhere in copy.
- **Don't** label the product or its data as an "example", "demo", or "sample".
- **Don't** show the product as screenshots or exported images. Rebuild what is needed in HTML.
- **Don't** put more than one app window, device frame, or feature grid on a page.
- **Don't** fill buttons, links, or headings with Ember Orange or Beam Blue.
- **Don't** use neutral black shadows inside the window, or shadows on paper beyond the email field.
- **Don't** add a second pinned stage, or any motion that ignores reduced-motion.
