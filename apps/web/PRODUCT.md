## Platform

web

## Users

People whose work is spread across many apps, sites, documents, meetings and AI tools. Most are on Windows. Many are not technical: a bakery owner who takes orders on WhatsApp has to understand in five seconds what Daylens does for them and why it is safe.

## Product Purpose

Daylens is a desktop app for Windows 11 and macOS that remembers which apps, windows and pages you used, so you and the AI you choose can find anything you saw and pick up where you left off. It writes your day as hours in plain words.

This site sells Daylens V2 before it ships in November: it explains the product, earns trust, and collects emails for the beta waitlist. Success is a visitor who understands it in five seconds, believes it is safe, joins the waitlist, and shares the page.

## Positioning

Local-only memory of your computer that works with any AI. Your history is stored only on your computer and Daylens never keeps a copy. It is open source (MIT), so anyone can check.

## Operating Context

- Windows ships through the Microsoft Store; macOS is a signed download.
- Channels: nightly (anyone, rough), beta (waitlist, invited in waves starting in November), stable after the beta.
- AI choice: Daylens AI, your own key, Claude Code or Codex on the machine, or none. Assistants read days over MCP.
- The site is hosted at christian-tonny.dev/daylens until Daylens has its own domain.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Daylens. App icon at public/app-icon.png.
- Voice: second person, plain words, short sentences. No hype words.
- Promise: "Find anything you saw, pick up where you left off."
- Trust headline: "Your history is stored only on your computer. We never keep a copy." Never "we never see it" unqualified: with Daylens AI each hour passes through the Daylens service to the model provider without being stored.
- Never name or compare to another product or company.
